About

I work at the intersection of business, data and technology

Professional summary

My role is not simply to build dashboards or data pipelines. I work with stakeholders to understand the decision or operational problem first, determine what information is required, assess the underlying data and then design a practical solution.

My experience spans enterprise finance analytics, ERP and business applications, telecommunications systems, data engineering and applied analytics across UK and Tanzanian environments.

Career journey

I started in telecommunications engineering, working with complex operational systems. The work moved toward data engineering and analytics, and today I work in group finance analytics and business applications inside an international engineering environment.

Current focus

Finance analytics, management reporting, Power BI, ERP/business applications and emerging Microsoft data technologies — with attention on what the organisation needs to know, improve or decide.

Where I’m heading

I am interested in organisations going through growth, digital transformation, reporting modernisation or operational change. That includes financial institutions and growing businesses. Independent consulting sits alongside — not instead of — this professional identity. Explore Consulting.
