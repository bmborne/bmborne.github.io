Areas I can support

Management Reporting & Analytics

  • Management dashboards
  • Finance reporting

Data, ERP & Business Information

For organisations where useful information is spread across ERP systems, operational applications, databases and disconnected reports.

  • Requirements analysis

  • UAT support
  • Data-quality assessment

Analytics, Automation & Applied AI

For organisations exploring forecasting, automation, document processing or anomaly detection.

Not every business problem requires AI. The starting point should be the business need, available data and expected value.

  • Opportunity assessment
  • Data readiness
  • Proof of concept
  • Forecasting
  • Anomaly detection
  • Governance considerations

Financial Services

I have a growing focus on how data and analytics can support banks, microfinance institutions and other financial organisations as they strengthen reporting, operational visibility and digital capabilities.

Areas where I am particularly interested in applying data and analytics include:

  • Management and executive reporting
  • Lending portfolio visibility
  • Branch performance
  • Finance and performance analytics
  • Data quality and reconciliation
  • Customer and transaction analytics
  • Operational exception monitoring
  • Forecasting
  • Document and workflow automation
  • Responsible AI assessment

Where sensitive financial information is involved, early assessments and demonstrations can use aggregated, anonymised or synthetic data where appropriate, before any production access is considered.

How I Work

  1. Understand Clarify the business problem, stakeholders and decision requirements.
  2. Assess Review existing data, systems, reporting and constraints.
  3. Design Define a practical solution.
  4. Prototype Demonstrate value at small scale where appropriate.
  5. Validate Test data, assumptions and outcomes.
  6. Implement / Support Develop or support the selected solution.
  7. Handover Document and support adoption.
