# Re-evaluation: the earlier assessment was too gentle

After reviewing the LinkedIn screenshots, the CV, the portfolio source code, the GitHub profile README and several repositories, my revised conclusion is:

> **Your main problem is not lack of technical capability or lack of qualifications. It is a combined positioning, evidence and trust problem.**

You are presenting a technically broad profile, but the different public channels do not describe the same professional. More importantly, several inconsistencies may cause a recruiter to question what is current, what is yours, what is production experience and what is academic or aspirational.

Your opportunity at discoverIE is genuinely strong. The company’s 2026 public reporting describes a FTSE 250 group with more than 30 operating businesses, approximately 4,600 employees across 21 countries and a decentralised, acquisition-led operating model. That makes group reporting, ERP integration and analytical standardisation a credible enterprise-transformation environment. ([discoverIE Group][1])

However:

> **Access to an enterprise environment does not automatically make someone a principal architect or senior consultant. The market will credit only the scope you owned, the outcomes you produced and the evidence you can defend.**

# 5. Your certification programme needs immediate control

The six programmes should not be treated equally.

CFI describes FMVA as approximately 100–120 hours in one comparison, while another CFI page estimates 120–200 hours. Its BIDA programme is separately estimated at around 150–200 hours. Therefore, FMVA and BIDA alone may require approximately 250–400 hours before accounting for PL-300, DP-600, DAMA, AI Business Professional, your master’s and live project delivery. ([Corporate Finance Institute][4])

Across approximately 30 weeks, ten study hours per week gives only 300 hours.

> **You cannot maximise your output by maximising your enrolments.**

## Revised certification decisions

| Programme                                       |       Fit with immediate career | Decision                                             | Required evidence alongside it                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------: | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PL-300**                                      |                       Very high | **Complete first**                                   | One reconciled, governed finance-reporting solution                        |
| **DP-600**                                      |                       Very high | **Complete second**                                  | A Fabric analytical architecture or controlled proof of concept            |
| **FMVA**                                        |                          Medium | **Continue at a controlled pace**                    | Driver-based forecast, financial model or business case                    |
| **DAMA/CDMP Associate learning**                |                     Medium-high | **Apply the material; defer the exam if necessary**  | KPI dictionary, lineage, data-quality rules and ownership model            |
| **BIDA**                                        | Low incremental value right now | **Pause**                                            | Revisit only after PL-300/DP-600 and the extension                         |
| **AI Business Professional**                    |                   Low-to-medium | **Optional quick supplement after core credentials** | AI opportunity-assessment framework and one controlled use case            |
| **MB-800**                                      |           Potentially very high | **Conditional third credential**                     | Genuine Business Central configuration, UAT, migration or process exposure |
| **Oracle FCC 2026 Implementation Professional** |                Potentially high | **Conditional employer-supported option**            | Actual FCC administration, configuration or integration responsibility     |

### PL-300

Microsoft describes PL-300 around preparing, modelling, visualising, analysing, managing and securing Power BI data, working with stakeholders and delivering understandable business value. That maps directly to your existing reporting work. ([Microsoft Learn][5])

### DP-600

DP-600 concerns enterprise analytical assets including semantic models, warehouses and lakehouses, together with stakeholder requirements, security and SQL/KQL/DAX. It should be paired with a real Fabric implementation or pilot rather than studied as theory. ([Microsoft Learn][6])

### FMVA

FMVA supports your long-term finance and investment objectives. It can strengthen:

* financial statements;
* forecasting;
* valuation;
* business cases;
* financial modelling;
* executive presentation.

But it is **not a substitute for group accounting, consolidation, controls or IFRS knowledge**.

For your current discoverIE work, financial-reporting literacy may eventually be better supplemented by targeted training in:

* group consolidation;
* management accounting;
* IFRS;
* foreign currency;
* intercompany eliminations;
* acquisition accounting;
* management reporting controls.

ACCA’s CertIFR, for example, is an online IFRS introduction typically requiring around 20 hours, but ACCA also says a financial-reporting background is recommended. It should therefore be considered only after discussion with a Group Finance mentor, not added automatically. ([ACCA Global][7])

### BIDA

BIDA includes Power Query, Power BI, DAX, SQL, Python, Tableau and general data analysis. Much of that overlaps with:

* your MSc;
* your data-engineering history;
* PL-300;
* DP-600;
* your existing analytics work.

CFI’s own programme information shows a broad curriculum and continuing programme changes. It would add workload more than differentiation during these seven months. ([Corporate Finance Institute][8])

**Do not automatically begin BIDA when FMVA ends.**

### AI Business Professional

Microsoft classifies AI Business Professional as a **beginner-level** credential for using Microsoft 365 Copilot and generative-AI productivity tools without coding or application development. It may help demonstrate corporate AI literacy, but it does not validate AI engineering, machine learning or production AI. ([Microsoft Learn][9])

Use it as a small supporting credential, not as a headline qualification.

### MB-800

MB-800 is more strategically relevant than BIDA where you are developing real Business Central responsibility. Microsoft’s current certification covers:

* financial management;
* sales and purchasing;
* inventory;
* fixed assets;
* company setup;
* data migration;
* implementation-team collaboration;
* reports, charts and integrations. ([Microsoft Learn][10])

Do not take it solely because Vertec uses Business Central. Take it when you can genuinely connect the syllabus to work you have performed.

### Oracle FCC

Oracle currently offers a Financial Consolidation and Close 2026 Implementation Professional certification. It could become differentiating because the Microsoft-and-Oracle combination is less common. However, it is appropriate only where your responsibilities extend beyond consuming FCC outputs into implementation, administration, configuration or integration. ([Oracle University][11])

### One correction to the previous plan

PL-200 should no longer be a future target because Microsoft is retiring the Power Platform Functional Consultant Associate certification and exam on 31 August 2026. ([Microsoft Learn][12])

Your recent anomaly-detection repository is substantially stronger than many older repositories. It has a package structure, tests, runbooks, safety controls and bronze/silver/gold data layers. That engineering discipline should become the standard for your future work.

However, it names TTCL, refers to two Zabbix environments, describes intended extraction controls and university data movement, and includes environment-variable structures for API and SSH access. The example file does not expose actual credentials, but the operational context may still create confidentiality or governance concerns.

Recommended action:

Your public GitHub should eventually pin only four to six repositories:

1. `finance-analytics-powerbi-reference`
2. `fabric-multi-entity-finance-platform`
3. `business-central-analytics-demo`
4. `ai-business-value-assessment`

* bootcamp exercises;

* empty repositories;
* generic week-one lab notebooks;

* projects with no useful README.

It does not provide meaningful evidence of your work and may confuse provenance when it is not presented as a fork. Archive it, remove it or fork the upstream project correctly.
